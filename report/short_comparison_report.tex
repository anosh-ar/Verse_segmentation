{\sffamily\bfseries\large VerSe test set: model trained on TotalSegmentator vs model trained on VerSe}

\input{comparison_table.tex}

\textbf{Findings.} Both models use the same network, training recipe and test set; only the training data
differ (902 TotalSegmentator scans at 1.5\,mm vs 56 VerSe19 training scans at 1\,mm). On a typical scan
both outline the vertebrae equally well (median Dice .93 vs .94), but the VerSe-trained model is clearly
worse on average (mean .76 vs .91), and L1 suffers most. The loss comes from \emph{naming} rather than
outlining: 7 scans are counted one level off (vs 1), and the model labels 72 whole vertebrae as lumbar that
are not (vs 26), mostly neck and thoracic vertebrae in the many VerSe scans that show the whole spine. With
only 56 scans, all cropped to the lumbar region, it has not learned well enough what is \emph{not} a lumbar
vertebra. Here, many training scans from another source beat few scans from the target source.
